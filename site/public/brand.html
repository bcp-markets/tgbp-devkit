Describe tGBP accurately

If your app or docs mention tGBP, use the wording below. Each claim matches what BCP Markets says about tGBP in its own terms and disclosures.

Name it

  • The full name is Tokenised GBP, with a British "s".
  • The ticker is tGBP: lower-case "t", capital "GBP". Never "TGBP" or "Tgbp".
  • The issuer is BCP Technologies Ltd, trading as BCP Markets.

Say this

  • tGBP is a British pound stablecoin.
  • Every tGBP token is backed one to one by cash and cash equivalents.
  • The reserves have independent monthly attestations and public on-chain supply evidence.
  • BCP Technologies Ltd is registered with the Financial Conduct Authority as a cryptoasset firm under the UK Money Laundering Regulations.
  • Eligible verified clients can redeem tGBP for pounds through the Tokenised GBP minting service.

Don't say this

AvoidWhy
"FCA-regulated stablecoin" or "FCA-authorised"The FCA registration covers the firm under the Money Laundering Regulations. It is not an authorisation of the token.
"e-money" or "electronic money"tGBP is not electronic money.
"protected" or "guaranteed"tGBP is not covered by the Financial Services Compensation Scheme.
"audited reserves"Say "independent monthly attestations".
"yield", "interest" or "earns"tGBP is not designed to pay a return or to gain in value.
"anyone can redeem"Redemption with BCP Markets is for eligible verified clients. Other routes depend on the exchange or partner.
"official partner" or "endorsed by tGBP"Using the developer kit does not make your project a partner.

Test tGBP

Test tGBP on Base Sepolia and other testnets has no value. It cannot be redeemed or bridged to a mainnet. Label it "test tGBP" wherever a user could mistake it for the real token.
